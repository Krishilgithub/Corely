How it works

From a question to a cited answer, in five steps.

This method is called Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG): search first, then let the model answer only from what was found.

Department of Computer Science & Engineering,
Devang Patel Institute of Advance Technology and Research (DEPSTAR)

Click through the five steps. One: Corely connects to the tools with OAuth and syncs their content; files can also be uploaded. Two: each document is split into pieces of about 400 words, with a 50-word overlap so meaning is not lost at the edges. Three: each piece is turned into a list of 1536 numbers that captures its meaning, stored in PostgreSQL with pgvector. Four: when someone asks a question, Corely finds the 8 closest pieces and keeps only those scoring above 0.55. Five: Claude writes the answer only from those pieces and cites each one. If nothing scores high enough, Corely says it does not know instead of guessing.
